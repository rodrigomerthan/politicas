Privacy Policy

Livre · Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what data the Livre app (the “App”), developed by Merthan’s Studio (“we”, “us”), collects, how that data is used, and what your rights are. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play policies.

Summary
  • The App has no sign-up and does not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We use Google services to show ads (AdMob), measure how the App is used (Firebase Analytics), and fix crashes (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change that choice at any time.
  • Your progress, your S.O.S. check-ins and your guardian friend’s details stay on your phone.
  • The website blocker is optional. It runs on your phone and does not send your browsing history to us.

2. Why we use data (legal bases)

  • Showing ads, which keep the App free. Personalized ads rely on your consent where the law requires it. Non-personalized ads rely on our legitimate interest.
  • Processing in-app purchases, to perform the purchase contract you entered into.
  • Measuring usage and fixing crashes, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and improving it.
  • Complying with legal obligations and preventing fraud.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Guardian friend and WhatsApp

If you choose a guardian friend, the App stores on your phone the name and WhatsApp number you type, and the code they create (hashed). This data is not sent to us.

When you tap "Message on WhatsApp", "Ask for prayer" or "Weekly report", the App only opens WhatsApp (or your browser) with a pre-written message to your friend. Nothing is sent automatically: you see the message and decide whether to send it. The report contains only what appears in the text (days free, urges overcome this week, record, and whether the blocker is on). Once sent, the message is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. Please ask your friend’s permission before adding their number.

5. Website blocker (local VPN)

The blocker is optional and only runs if you turn it on. To block adult websites across the phone, it uses Android’s VPN feature (VpnService) entirely on the device. What it does and does not do:

  • It only looks at the names of the sites your phone looks up (DNS queries, such as “example.com”). It does not see page content, messages, passwords or any other data you send.
  • Names on the App’s adult-content list are blocked on the phone itself. Google, Bing and YouTube searches are directed to those services’ official safe mode.
  • Other lookups are answered by Cloudflare’s “1.1.1.1 for Families” DNS service (1.1.1.3), which also filters adult content. As with any DNS service, Cloudflare receives the looked-up site name and your connection’s IP address, under the 1.1.1.1 resolver privacy policy, which commits to not selling this data and to deleting logs within 25 hours.
  • The App keeps only counts on the phone: how many visits were blocked, how many attempts there were, and how many times protection was turned off outside the App. When a site is blocked, the App shows a discreet notification ("Site blocked by Livre") that does not name the site. We do not store the list of sites you visited, and none of this is sent to us.
  • Turning the blocker off may require your guardian’s code. You can also turn it off at any time in Android’s VPN settings or by uninstalling the App.

6. Consent and ad choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where the law requires it, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before using data for personalized ads. You can:

  • Review or change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy options in the App;
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID on Android under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the path may vary by device);
  • Buy the ad-removal option, if the App offers it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for up to 2 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: follow the retention periods set in Google’s policies.
  • Data stored on your device (such as preferences and purchase status) is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

8. International transfers

The providers above may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. In those cases, Google applies the safeguards required by the LGPD and the GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) and the GDPR, you may request: confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization, restriction or deletion, portability, information about who we share it with, and withdrawal of consent. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interest and complain to Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Because the App has no user accounts, we cannot link data to you by name. To make a request, email us at the address in section 13. If possible, include the App name and your device model. We reply within 15 days.

10. Data deletion

You can delete data stored on your device by uninstalling the App or under Settings › Apps › Livre › Storage › Clear data. To request deletion of data collected by the services in section 3, contact us at the email in section 13. Purchases remain linked to your Google account and can be restored later.

11. Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security and changes

Data is transmitted with encryption (HTTPS) and stored by the providers with appropriate security measures. We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will announce significant changes in the App or on its store page.
